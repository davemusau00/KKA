# Product vision

The public website is the acquisition, publishing and trust layer of the same digital ecosystem as the KKA LawFirm OS. It is **not** an OS skin.

## Public goals
- Establish authority and trust through a premium partner-led presentation.
- Explain practice areas without jargon overload.
- Turn media into a measurable business-development channel.
- Capture enquiries as leads, not unstructured email.
- Route qualified prospects into formal OS intake without retyping.
- Allow authorised staff to publish and maintain the site without source-code changes.

## Experience character
Majestic, architectural, editorial, legal, restrained, human, Kenyan and contemporary. Navy provides institutional depth; ivory provides editorial breathing room; champagne gold works as a controlled accent; classical serif display type is paired with precise modern body typography.

## Non-goal
The public site must never look like the internal enterprise dashboard. Shared infrastructure is invisible to visitors.
